SWCorp app family

Every internal SWCorp web app is one floating card: navy header, white body, one scrolling region, actions pinned at the bottom. This page is built with swcorp.css only, so what you see here is what an app gets by copying it. Rules and reasons live in CLAUDE.md.

Color

Navy is the only brand color. Greys lower volume; they never carry meaning. Meaning ladder: info navy · pending amber · warning · ok green · blocking red. Red only for hard failures.

Toasts and dialog

Every floating message is a toast in one stack (.toast-stack), bottom-right, top on phones. One card for all: 3px edge by meaning — info navy, .warn amber (wrong file, something still missing), .error red (hard failures only), .ok green. Every toast leaves after 6s; the line along its foot shows the time left and pauses on hover, focus or a hidden tab. A pending action ("Additional Files Required") also leaves as soon as it is done. Never top-centre, never a bar inside the screen. Dialog: native <dialog>, blurred navy backdrop, springy entrance.
